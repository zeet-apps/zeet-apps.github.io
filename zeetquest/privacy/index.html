Privacy Policy – ZeetQuest

Last updated: October 12, 2026

ZeetQuest: Quiz for All, previously named Brain Boost, is a quiz app published on Google Play and the Apple App Store by Zeet Apps, the developer name of Balaji Sivakumar, an individual developer based in Ontario, Canada ("Zeet Apps", "we"). This policy explains how the app handles information. The app stores your choices and progress locally and does not send them to Zeet Apps. Check your store's current content rating when choosing the app for a child.

Information the app keeps

  • A nickname you choose, so the app can greet you. It does not need to be your real name.
  • An age group (for example 5–7, 8–11, 12–17 or 18 and over) that you pick, so the app can show suitable quizzes. We never ask for a date of birth.
  • Your progress: which quizzes and levels you have finished and your scores.

This information is stored in the app's local preferences. Change your age group using Settings → Who is playing? To clear your nickname, age group and progress, use Settings → Reset All Data; the app then starts setup again. Zeet Apps cannot access or recover this information.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, transmit or sell any personal information.
  • The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The app works offline, has no in-app purchases and does not upload your quiz data.

Children's privacy

Zeet Apps does not collect children's personal information through the app. A nickname does not need to be a real name, and we ask for an age group rather than a date of birth. Parents and guardians can remove the saved information using Reset All Data in Settings.

Device backups

Your operating system may include the app's local preferences in device backups or device transfers, depending on your settings. Apple or Google handles those services under its own privacy policy; Zeet Apps does not receive those backups. Manage or delete device backups through your device or cloud-account settings. Reset All Data clears the current app's data, but does not delete an existing backup.

Support and this website

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include so we can respond. Please do not send sensitive personal information. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages; the hosting provider may process technical request information under GitHub's privacy statement. This is separate from the offline quiz app.

Security

Data is kept only in the app's private storage on your device, so it is protected by your device's own security. We cannot see it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above.
