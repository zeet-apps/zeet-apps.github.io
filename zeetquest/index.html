ZeetQuest: Quiz for All

Previously Brain Boost. Short quizzes for curious minds. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.

About

ZeetQuest has thousands of multiple-choice questions across more than twenty subjects: animals, countries and flags, science and space, maths, language, history, music, food and more. Pick a nickname and an age group, and the app shows quizzes and levels that suit you. Finish a level to earn stars and unlock the next one.

  • Choose an age group for tailored quizzes, including harder questions for teens and adults. Check your store's content rating before choosing the app for a child.
  • Works on phones and tablets, online or offline
  • Your nickname, age group and progress are stored locally; Zeet Apps does not receive them. Device backups depend on your operating-system settings.
