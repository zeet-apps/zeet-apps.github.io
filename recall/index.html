How to save a link

Save links from YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, Twitter / X, Reddit, browsers and other apps that share a URL. In ZeetRecall, tap Save Link and paste a link, or use the sharing steps below.

ZeetRecall detects the link's domain and groups recognized sources together. Links from other websites appear under Other; they are not grouped into a separate collection for every domain. Add your own tags and notes to organize them further.

Titles and preview images appear when the website provides them. Private or login-required posts may not provide a preview; you can still save their link. Saving a link does not download the video or make private content accessible.

On iPhone or iPad, use Share in Safari, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram or another app that shares a URL, then choose ZeetRecall. Enable it under More if needed. Add optional tags, tap Save, then return to ZeetRecall to see your link. The Share Extension saves into the profile selected in the app.

On Android, use Share in YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, your browser or another app that shares a URL, choose ZeetRecall, then finish saving it in the app.

Your collections, on your device

No sign-up is required. Saved links, notes, tags and profile names are stored locally. Profiles organize separate collections on the same device; they are not password-protected accounts. There is no cloud sync or backup export/import in the current release.

To clear every profile's local content, open Settings > Backup & data > Delete all local data. Type DELETE and tap Delete everything to confirm. This cannot be undone within the app and does not remove external backups or data held by advertising providers.

Free with advertising

ZeetRecall is free with Google AdMob integration. To load previews, your device requests the linked webpage and preview images from their hosts. Those services receive normal network request information, including your IP address. ZeetRecall does not pass saved links, notes, tags or profile names as advertising targeting data. Google AdMob's SDK can automatically collect and share IP addresses (including approximate location), device/app identifiers where available, app and ad interactions, advertising data, and crash, diagnostic and performance information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Consent and device privacy settings affect data handling; declining tracking does not stop all SDK collection. Read the privacy policy for details. No purchase is required to use the app.
