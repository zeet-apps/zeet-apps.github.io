ZeetRecall

Save the link. Keep your thoughts. Find it when you need it.

A save-for-later app by Balaji Sivakumar, an independent developer in Canada publishing as Zeet Apps.

A home for the links you want to revisit

Share a link from your browser or another app, or paste it directly into ZeetRecall. Add a personal note and tags to remember why it matters. Browse by source or tag, search your collection, and open the original page when you're ready.

  • Save links from browsers and apps that support sharing URLs
  • Add and edit notes and tags; search titles, URLs, notes and tags
  • Browse by source or tag, and archive saved items
  • Create local profiles with separate saves, notes and tags
  • Delete individual saves or clear all local content with confirmation

How to save a link

In ZeetRecall, tap Save Link, paste a public webpage URL, add optional details and save. Page titles and images appear when the website provides them; you can still save a link without a preview.

On iPhone or iPad, open a webpage in Safari, tap Share and choose ZeetRecall. Enable it under More if needed. Add optional tags, tap Save, then return to ZeetRecall to see your link. The Share Extension saves into the profile selected in the app.

On Android, share a URL to ZeetRecall and finish saving it in the app.

Your collections, on your device

No sign-up is required. Saved links, notes, tags and profile names are stored locally. Profiles organize separate collections on the same device; they are not password-protected accounts. There is no cloud sync or backup export/import in the current release.

To clear every profile's local content, open Settings > Backup & data > Delete all local data. Type DELETE and tap Delete everything to confirm. This cannot be undone within the app and does not remove external backups or data held by advertising providers.

Free with advertising

ZeetRecall is free with Google AdMob integration. Ads may not always be available. Link previews contact the linked websites and image hosts, and advertising services may process device and usage data. Read the privacy policy for details. No purchase is required to use the app.
