ZeetRecall

Save links from any app, add notes, and find them later.

About

ZeetRecall captures links shared from other apps (Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, Reddit and more), lets you attach your own notes and tags, and searches across everything. Your saved items stay on your device.

  • Share a link into ZeetRecall from your browser or another app
  • Add notes and tags, then search everything
  • No account needed; your content is stored locally on your device
  • Free, supported by ads
