Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the ZeetRecall app (developed by Balaji Sivakumar, publishing as Zeet Apps in Canada) collects, uses, and protects information when you use the app.

Information We Collect

ZeetRecall lets you save links, notes, and tags for later. The content you save — URLs, titles, notes, tags, and local profile names — is:

  • Entered or shared by you (typed directly, or shared into the app from another app such as a browser or social media app)
  • Stored locally on your device in the app's own database
  • Not uploaded to a ZeetRecall server for storage or sync. Preview requests send the saved URL to the linked website and fetch images from their hosts, as described below

ZeetRecall does not require you to create an account, and does not ask for your name, email address, or any other personal identifier to use its core save/organize features.

Third-Party Services

ZeetRecall is free and supported by ads. It integrates Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK and uses Google's User Messaging Platform for advertising consent. ZeetRecall does not pass saved links, notes, tags or profile names as advertising targeting data. This does not prevent the SDK from collecting its own device, network and interaction data.

ServicePurposeData involved
Google AdMob Shows ads The SDK can automatically collect and share IP addresses (including IP-derived approximate location), device/app identifiers such as advertising ID and app set ID where available, app and ad interactions, advertising data, and crash, diagnostic and performance information. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Identifier availability and data handling depend on platform, permissions, consent and SDK settings. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's Android and iOS SDK disclosures.

Advertising choices: the app uses Google's consent flow, which can show a consent form where required. On iOS, the app requests App Tracking Transparency permission; access to IDFA requires authorization. Android advertising-ID controls are available in device settings. Declining tracking or personalized advertising does not necessarily stop all SDK collection: IP addresses, diagnostics and other information may still be processed. These choices do not require an account or purchase to use core features.

Link previews: your device requests the linked webpage, follows applicable redirects, and fetches preview images from their hosts. These services receive the requested URL and normal network request information, including your IP address. URLs themselves may contain identifying information. Preview data is cached on the device. Saved notes, tags and profile names are not added to preview requests.

Permissions

ZeetRecall requests internet access (to fetch link titles and preview images and to load ads), access to the advertising identifier on Android (for ads), and, on iOS, the ability to receive content shared from other apps via the system Share Sheet and, if you allow it, tracking permission for ads. ZeetRecall does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, or location.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your saved content or upload your collections to our own server. Preview requests disclose the requested URL to the destination website and image hosts. Google AdMob collects and may share advertising-related information as described above and in its policies. Opening a saved link or an ad also contacts the destination service, whose privacy policy applies.

Data Retention and Deletion

Saved items and local profiles remain in the app's local storage until deleted or the app's data is removed. Delete individual saves within the app, or open Settings > Backup & data > Delete all local data, type DELETE and tap Delete everything. This removes app-owned local saves, notes, tags, profiles, pending shares and cached previews and resets to an empty Default profile. It cannot be undone within the app. External backups and information held by Google or other services are not removed by this cleanup. Those services apply their own retention/deletion policies.

Profiles are local collections without passwords or online accounts; anyone with access to the unlocked app can switch profiles. There is no cloud sync or backup export/import in this release and no online ZeetRecall account to delete.

Children's Privacy

ZeetRecall is a general-audience productivity app and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the relevant age of digital consent in your region). If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will address it.

Your Rights

Since your saved content lives on your device, you have direct control over it at all times — you can view, edit, or delete it within the app, or remove everything by uninstalling. If you contact support, we receive the email address and information you choose to send and use them to respond. Avoid including private saved content. For data held by Google for advertising, see Google's privacy policy and your device's ad settings.

Future Changes

Future versions may add an optional one-time purchase to remove ads, or optional sync between your devices. If so, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe exactly what is collected and by whom before that version is released, and any required consent will be requested in the app.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices. If we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
