Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the ZeetRecall app (developed by Zeet Apps) collects, uses, and protects information when you use the app.

Information We Collect

ZeetRecall lets you save links, notes, and tags for later. The content you save — URLs, titles, notes, and tags — is:

  • Entered or shared by you (typed directly, or shared into the app from another app such as a browser or social media app)
  • Stored locally on your device in the app's own database
  • Not sent to our servers or any third party as part of normal use

ZeetRecall does not require you to create an account, and does not ask for your name, email address, or any other personal identifier to use its core save/organize features.

Third-Party Services

ZeetRecall is free and supported by ads. It uses the following third-party service. It never receives the content you save (your links, notes, or tags).

ServicePurposeData involved
Google AdMob Shows ads Advertising identifier and standard mobile ad request data (such as device and app information and an approximate location derived from your IP address), per Google's Privacy Policy. Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK) you are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and on iOS the system asks permission before the advertising identifier is used for tracking. You can decline, or reset or limit the advertising identifier in your device settings; ads will then be less relevant.

Link previews: when you save a link, ZeetRecall contacts that link's own website to fetch its page title and preview image, the same way a web browser would. That website can see the request. Previews are cached on your device.

Permissions

ZeetRecall requests internet access (to fetch link titles and preview images and to load ads), access to the advertising identifier on Android (for ads), and, on iOS, the ability to receive content shared from other apps via the system Share Sheet and, if you allow it, tracking permission for ads. ZeetRecall does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, or location.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your data and we do not share the links, notes, or tags you save with anyone. Advertising data is collected by Google AdMob as described above.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your saved items are stored locally on your device for as long as the app is installed. You can delete individual items at any time within the app, or remove all data by uninstalling the app. ZeetRecall does not currently store your saved content on any server, so there is no separate account to delete.

Children's Privacy

ZeetRecall is a general-audience productivity app and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the relevant age of digital consent in your region). If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will address it.

Your Rights

Since your saved content lives on your device, you have direct control over it at all times — you can view, edit, or delete it within the app, or remove everything by uninstalling. We hold no personal data about you. For data held by Google for advertising, see Google's privacy policy and your device's ad settings.

Future Changes

Future versions may add an optional one-time purchase to remove ads, or optional sync between your devices. If so, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe exactly what is collected and by whom before that version is released, and any required consent will be requested in the app.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices. If we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
