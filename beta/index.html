Beta testing

Help me shape apps before they are released. Pick an app and your phone, send me a short email, and I will send you an invitation.

How it works

  • Android: I add the email you use for Google Play to a closed test. You get a link, accept it, and install from Google Play.
  • iPhone: I invite you to TestFlight, Apple's free testing app. Install TestFlight first, then open my invitation.
  • What I ask: use the app as you normally would and send me what felt confusing, broken or missing.
