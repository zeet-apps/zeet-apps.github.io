Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Againlings app (developed by Balaji Sivakumar, publishing as Zeet Apps in Canada) collects, uses, and protects information when you play the game.

Information We Collect

Againlings is a puzzle platformer. The only information the game keeps about you is your progress:

  • Which levels you have finished, the stars you earned, your best times, your fewest runs, and the level you opened last
  • Stored locally on your device in the app's own storage
  • Not uploaded to an Againlings server. We do not run a server for the game

Againlings does not require you to create an account, and does not ask for your name, email address, or any other personal identifier. The game has no analytics or crash-reporting service of its own. The Daily level is built on your device from the calendar date, so it does not use the network.

Third-Party Services

Againlings is free and supported by ads. It integrates Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK and uses Google's User Messaging Platform for advertising consent. Your progress is not passed to advertisers or used as advertising targeting data. This does not prevent the SDK from collecting its own device, network and interaction data.

ServicePurposeData involved
Google AdMob Shows ads The SDK can automatically collect and share IP addresses (including IP-derived approximate location), device/app identifiers such as advertising ID and app set ID where available, app and ad interactions, advertising data, and crash, diagnostic and performance information. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Identifier availability and data handling depend on platform, permissions, consent and SDK settings. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's Android and iOS SDK disclosures.

Advertising choices: the game uses Google's consent flow, which shows a consent form where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland) before any ad is requested. On iOS, the game requests App Tracking Transparency permission; access to the advertising identifier (IDFA) requires your authorization. Android advertising-ID controls are available in device settings. Declining tracking or personalized advertising does not necessarily stop all SDK collection: IP addresses, diagnostics and other information may still be processed. These choices do not require an account or purchase to play.

When ads appear: an ad can appear between levels, after you finish one. Ads are not shown while you are playing a level.

Permissions

Againlings requests internet access (to load ads) and access to the advertising identifier on Android (for ads). On iOS it asks for tracking permission for ads if you allow it. Againlings does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your information, and we do not receive your progress. Google AdMob collects and may share advertising-related information as described above and in its policies. Opening an ad contacts the advertiser's destination, whose privacy policy applies.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your progress remains on your device until you uninstall the game or clear its data in your device settings. There is no online Againlings account to delete. Information held by Google or other services is not removed by uninstalling; those services apply their own retention and deletion policies, and Google's tools are at myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy.

Children's Privacy

Againlings is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant age of digital consent in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we will address it.

Your Rights

Because your progress lives on your device, you have direct control over it at all times. You can decline consent, reset your advertising ID, or uninstall the game. If you contact support, we receive the email address and information you choose to send and use them to respond. For data held by Google for advertising, see Google's privacy policy and your device's ad settings.

Future Changes

Future versions may add an optional one-time purchase to remove ads, or rewarded ads that you choose to watch for a hint. If so, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe exactly what is collected and by whom before that version is released, and any required consent will be requested in the game.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices. If we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
